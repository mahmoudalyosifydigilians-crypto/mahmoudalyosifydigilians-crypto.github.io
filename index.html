Printing & cutting guide
  • The PDF has 2 pages: page 1 = 3 fronts, page 2 = 3 backs (QR).
  • Paper: A4 · Scale: 100% / Default · Margins: None · Background graphics: on.
  • Two-sided: choose Print on both sides → Flip on long edge.
  • No two-sided printer? Print page 1, put the same sheet back in the tray flipped over, then print page 2 — or print both pages and glue them back to back.
  • Cut the left and right margins along the marks first, then cut between the cards — 3 cards per sheet.
  • Use thick paper (250–300 gsm) and scan the QR with your phone before printing many copies.
